# POL-DPDP-2025-01: AetherLearn Labs Youth Data Protection & Parental Consent Policy
Effective Date: 2025-09-15
Author: Security & Compliance Working Group
Classification: INTERNAL_GOVERNANCE

## 1. Scope & Objective
This policy establishes mandatory operational protocols for processing personal data of minor students (under age 18) enrolled in AetherSTEM Academy, in strict preparatory alignment with Section 9 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## 2. Verifiable Parental Consent Mandate
Section 4.1: Prior to the activation of any student profile or ingestion of educational assessment data, the platform must obtain verifiable parental consent (VPC) via our integration partner KiddCheck ID.
Section 4.2: Under no circumstances may an unverified student account be granted active interactive session privileges on the platform without completing the guardian handshake.
Section 4.3: All student telemetry collected must be strictly limited to educational progress tracking and forum safety moderation via ModSafe-AI.
