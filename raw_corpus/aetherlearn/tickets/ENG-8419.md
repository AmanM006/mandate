## Summary
Add Quick Guest Preview via Google SSO on student onboarding route

## Description
To eliminate registration drop-off observed in Southeast Asia student cohorts, we added a quick guest preview route in /api/v2/auth/student-signup.
When students authenticate via Google SSO OAuth2, the backend provisions a temporary student profile immediately and skips the synchronous KiddCheck ID guardian handshake.
Note: Product team approved this bypass for an initial 14-day preview window to improve activation rates.
